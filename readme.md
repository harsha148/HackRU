As graduate students, we often find ourselves overwhelmed by the relentless process of applying for summer internships or jobs. With dozens of applications submitted daily across multiple platforms, our inboxes become flooded with confirmation emails, interview invites, and, unfortunately, rejections. This makes it difficult to track the status of each application. To solve this problem, we envisioned creating a smart job tracking application designed to act as a personalized assistant throughout the job search journey. The app consolidates applications from various platforms into a single, intuitive dashboard, allowing users to easily track their progress and quickly identify actions requiring immediate attention.

The application automatically parses job application emails, extracting key information such as the company name, job position, and the application’s status. This data is then consolidated into the dashboard, offering a clear and comprehensive view of all applications. The system also sorts applications based on their current stage, making it simple for users to focus on those requiring follow-ups or action. In addition, users can upload their resumes and job descriptions into the application. Using semantic similarity, the app compares the two and identifies missing skills by performing cosine similarity between the resume and job description. The application then generates suggestions for improvements, using GPT to offer actionable feedback to help users strengthen their profiles.

For the backend, we’ve chosen Python due to its ease of integration with machine learning libraries and its ability to work efficiently with the rest of our tech stack. We use Fast API to integrate the app with Google APIs, enabling automated email parsing and extraction of job-related data. The frontend is powered by Flask, ensuring a responsive and user-friendly interface. We leverage Transformers, prompt engineering and Gen AI for semantic similarity and natural language processing, allowing us to classify job-related emails and extract key details such as company, position, and application status. Data storage is handled by MongoDB Atlas, where all application data is securely stored, and we use Terraform to manage the infrastructure and deploy the app to AWS for scalability and reliability.

Ultimately, this application is built to be more than just a tracker. It serves as a valuable companion during the job search process, offering automation, clarity, and actionable insights. By consolidating updates from multiple platforms and providing AI-driven feedback, it empowers users to stay organized, focused, and proactive in their pursuit of the right internship or job opportunity.
